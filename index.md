## About

I'm a PhD student in biochemistry and molecular biophysics at Caltech. I'm currently in Stockholm as a Fulbright U.S. Student Researcher at the Karolinska Institutet, hosted by Per Svenningsson.

My work is on receptor pharmacology and the computational methods used to find ligands — building and filtering libraries, docking, and learned scoring functions that decide what gets made. Before Stockholm I worked on cryo-EM structural biology of opioid receptors with Cornelius Gati at USC, where I studied chemistry as an undergrad. I was also an NIH Amgen Scholar at NICHD in 2023.

If you're interested in working together, feel free to reach out by email.
