## About

I am a Ph.D. student in biochemistry and molecular biophysics at [Caltech](https://cce.caltech.edu/) (2024–present), and I am spending this year in Stockholm as a [Fulbright](https://us.fulbrightonline.org/) U.S. Student Researcher at the [Karolinska Institutet](https://ki.se/en), hosted by [Prof. Per Svenningsson](https://ki.se/en/people/per-svenningsson) (2026–27).

Before Caltech I studied chemistry at [USC](https://dornsife.usc.edu/chem/) (2021–2024), where I worked with [Prof. Cornelius Gati](https://gatilab.com/) on the structural biology of opioid receptors, and spent a summer as an Amgen Scholar at the [NIH/NICHD](https://www.nichd.nih.gov/) with Dr. Sergey Bezrukov (2023). I hold the David Baltimore–Amgen Graduate Fellowship (2025).

My current research is on receptor pharmacology: how receptors recognize the molecules that act on them, and how that understanding becomes a new drug. I am always glad to hear from people thinking about similar problems — <osalah@caltech.edu>.
